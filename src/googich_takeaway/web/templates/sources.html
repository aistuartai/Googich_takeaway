How exports arrive

Add a Google Drive folder

Guide: Connecting Google Drive
  1. In Takeout, choose Add to Drive as the delivery method (Setting up Google Takeout).
  2. Create a service account with read-only access, and download its key (the guide walks through it).
  3. Share the Takeout folder in Drive with the service account, as Viewer, then add it here.

Use archives you download

Guide: Downloading exports yourself
  1. In Takeout, choose Send download link via email as the delivery method.
  2. When the email arrives, download every part (…-001.zip, …-002.zip and so on) within a week, before the links expire.
  3. Save them into the folder below. The next run imports the export once all its parts are there and none has changed for half an hour.
