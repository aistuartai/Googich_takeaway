Google Drive

This app never deletes anything in Google Drive. Below are the Takeout archives it downloaded, and whether each export is safe to remove. To remove one: press Check Immich now, open each archive's link, delete it in Drive, then empty Drive's trash, because trashed files still count against your storage.

Removing archives from Drive does not touch Google Photos. Once removed, your copies are Google Photos and Immich's storage: make sure Immich's storage is backed up.
